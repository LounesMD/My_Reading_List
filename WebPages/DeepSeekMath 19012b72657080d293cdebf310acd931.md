# DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models

### Tokenizer:

- Not specified in the paper.

### Data Collection:

- The majority of the data comes from **Common Crawl** (~200B tokens).
- Additional math-specific data is scraped using the following process:
    - **OpenWebMath** as a source.
    - **Seed Corpus:** They start with a small corpus of high quality math-related tokens.
    - **FastText Model:**
        - Trained to classify new data based on the math seed corpus (discriminative between data from math seed vs common crowl).
        - They retain the top-K results (classify as math data).
        - If a large portion of the data comes from the same URL domain, they reconsider the content from that entire domain.
    - This process is **iterative:** Extend the math seed, retrain, and loop.
- **Dataset Validation:**
    - They train an LLM on various datasets to verify the relevance and quality of the created dataset.

### (Pre) Training:

- **Base Model:** A 7B model based on the **DeepSeek Coder** architecture.
- **Training Data:** A mix of their math dataset combined with other datasets (both English and Chinese).
- They **pass through their dataset twice** during training.
- The model achieves SOTA compared to models like **LLaMA**, **Mistral**, and **Minerva**.

### Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT):

- A fine-tuning dataset is constructed (including both English and Chinese content).
- The **Instruct model** is fine-tuned for **500 steps**.

### Reinforcement Learning Fine-Tuning (RLFT):

- The RL process involves (**TRPO → PPO →) GRPO** (Generalized Reward Policy Optimization).
- **Policy Learning:**
    - The policy is parameterized, aiming to learn it similarly to Q-Learning.
    - **Weight Update Formula:**
    Δθ = -log(π(obs)) * A
    where A = (reward - baseline).
- **Reward Model:**
    - The reward is computed using both the fine-tuning target model and the reference SFT model.
    - A **penalty term** is introduced to adjust the reward, preventing the model from deviating too far from the reference.
    - The reward model is trained to **rank answer quality**, though the paper lacks detailed explanations.
- **GRPO vs PPO:**
    - Unlike PPO, **GRPO does not require a value model**.
    - Instead of using a value model, it relies on the **mean reward** from multiple observations.

Extra Notes:

- **Chain of Thought (CoT):**
    
    If intermediate steps can be rewarded, CoT reasoning can be effectively encouraged.
    
- **Performance Metrics:**
    
    RL improves **M@K (Majority@K)** performance. However, it does **not improve Pass@K** scores.
